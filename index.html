DIFFERENT WAYS TO LIVE

At The Rise, every level introduces a different way to experience life.

From landscaped gardens and family spaces at the ground level to wellness retreats on the terrace and vibrant social destinations above, every amenity has been thoughtfully placed to create a seamless rhythm between recreation, relaxation, work, and community.

WELL CONNECTED. WELL POSITIONED.

Located at Budigere Cross, The Rise offers seamless access to Bengaluru's most important destinations while remaining comfortably removed from the city's everyday congestion.

With excellent connectivity to Whitefield, KR Puram, NH75, the upcoming metro network, and Kempegowda International Airport, every journey becomes shorter, simpler, and more convenient.
